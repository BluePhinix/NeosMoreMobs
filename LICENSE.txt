--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Model: "Minecraft GrizzlyBear"
Author: ContinuedOak
Source: https://skfb.ly/o8yAw
License: Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International
         (CC BY-NC 4.0)
License URL: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/

Changes made:
- Imported the model into Minecraft Bedrock geometry.
- Preserved the original UV mapping and texture layout.
- Split the model into body, head, ear, leg, and tail bones for animation.
- Added a small tail cube.
- Recolored the texture for the Black Bear variant.
- Authored new idle, walk, run, and attack animations.

must not be sold or used commercially while this model is included.
